## Ideation summary (seed content to paste)

**Problem:** In Addis Ababa trauma care, relatives and bystanders frequently provide scene care; lack of knowledge is the leading reported reason care is not given. Professional EMS and digital coordination platforms (e.g. HEARTS, RapidSOS-class tools) do not fully solve what the untrained person at the scene should do safely.

**Approach:** Voice-first assistant that (1) understands natural speech, (2) maintains structured emergency state, (3) allows only protocol-approved guidance, (4) produces a responder handoff.

**Non-goals:** AI diagnosis, dispatch replacement, ambulance fleet management, unrestricted medical chatbot.
